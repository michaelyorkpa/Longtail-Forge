# Attorney Review Checklist

This checklist is for a future attorney review before Longtail Forge accepts non-trivial contributors, sells paid hosting, sells managed private instances, grants commercial license exceptions, or transfers assets into an LLC.

## Licensing Stack

Ask counsel to review:

- AGPL-3.0-only for the public core;
- Apache-2.0 for plugin SDKs and integration examples;
- CC BY 4.0 for documentation prose;
- proprietary/all-rights-reserved treatment for private SaaS and commercial plugins;
- whether current repo notices clearly avoid `AGPL-3.0-or-later`;
- whether the root `LICENSE`, README, source headers, and docs agree.

## Contributor Rights

Ask counsel to review:

- contributor policy;
- draft CLA;
- whether the CLA grants sufficient copyright rights for dual licensing and commercial exceptions;
- whether the CLA patent grant is sufficient;
- electronic signature/acceptance process;
- CLA record retention;
- treatment of employer-owned contributions;
- treatment of AI-assisted contributions;
- contractor/freelancer agreement requirements.

## Trademark

Ask counsel to review:

- Longtail Forge name availability;
- Raymond Tec ownership posture;
- whether/when to file federal trademark application;
- trademark policy enforceability;
- use of "LTF" as shorthand;
- domain/social/package/app-name strategy;
- permitted fork/reference language.

## Current DBA and Future Entity

Ask counsel/accountant to review:

- current DBA/trade-name status;
- EIN/tax setup;
- whether paid hosting can initially operate under Michael York d/b/a Raymond Tec;
- when to form a Pennsylvania LLC;
- whether Raymond Tec LLC or Longtail Forge LLC should own the IP;
- IP assignment documents;
- trademark assignment documents;
- customer contract assignment path;
- bank/payment processor setup.

## Paid Hosting / SaaS

Before paid hosting, ask counsel to prepare or review:

- Terms of Service;
- Privacy Policy;
- Acceptable Use Policy;
- Data Processing Addendum if needed;
- customer data ownership language;
- admin/support access policy;
- backup/restore policy;
- service-level/support policy;
- cancellation/deletion/export policy;
- limitation of liability;
- warranty disclaimer;
- indemnity position;
- payment/refund policy;
- tax collection exposure;
- security incident notification terms.

## Managed Private Instances

Ask counsel to review:

- managed service agreement;
- statement of work template;
- support scope;
- uptime/support disclaimers or commitments;
- customer access/control terms;
- backup obligations;
- update obligations;
- data return/deletion at termination;
- customer-specific customization ownership;
- commercial license exception language if needed.

## Commercial License Exceptions

Ask counsel to prepare:

- standard commercial license exception template;
- pricing/term boundaries;
- restrictions on sublicensing/resale;
- source-code escrow position if any;
- audit/enforcement language;
- termination language;
- treatment of third-party dependencies;
- customer confidentiality terms.

## Dependency and Asset Compliance

Ask counsel or a license specialist to review:

- dependency inventory;
- third-party notices;
- license scanner output;
- bundled fonts/icons/images;
- generated assets;
- copied snippets;
- examples/templates;
- compatibility of any MPL/LGPL/GPL/AGPL third-party components;
- whether SDK Apache-2.0 boundaries are clean.

## Privacy and Security

Ask counsel/security advisor to review:

- handling of user data;
- business/customer data;
- encrypted notes/security claims;
- API keys;
- authentication;
- audit logs;
- backup retention;
- deletion/export;
- breach response;
- third-party processors;
- privacy policy claims.

## Exit Readiness

Before investment, sale, or acquisition discussions, prepare:

- IP ownership schedule;
- repository list;
- domain/account list;
- trademark evidence;
- contributor/CLA records;
- contractor agreements;
- license exception agreements;
- customer contracts;
- dependency inventory;
- security/privacy policies;
- financial records;
- entity assignment documents.
