## The problem

**Heading:** The hard part isn't the task. It's getting back into it.

Real client work rarely happens start to finish. You get interrupted, switch clients, and come back hours later to a task you can barely remember starting. The details — what was in progress, the next step, why it was blocked, where the file is, how much time you already logged — have scattered across tabs, notes, email, and memory.

That reconstruction tax adds up. Longtail Forge is built to cut it.

## Workbench

**Heading:** Dashboard tells you what's happening. Workbench helps you begin.

**Body:** Workbench is where you pick your work back up. It surfaces what you were doing, points you at a useful next action, and keeps the connected context — your resume note, the linked note, the attached file, the timer — right there when you return. No digging through twenty lists to remember where you were.

- Focus selection that points you at the work worth resuming.
- Task Focus with the next action, resume note, and connected context in one place.

## Connected modules

**Heading:** Your work, kept together.

Longtail Forge keeps different kinds of work in distinct but connected places, so nothing drifts away from the job it belongs to. **Available today:**

- **Tasks** — commitments with next actions, blockers, checklists, reminders, and time history.
- **Notes** — working memory, decisions, and research, with optional encrypted-at-rest secure notes.
- **Lists** — repeatable operational checklists and reusable workflows.
- **Files** — supporting artifacts attached to the record where the work happens.
- **Time Tracking** — track time on the work itself, ready for billing and reporting.
- **Search & Notifications** — recover information across your work and surface relevant changes.
- **Reporting** — understand billable and non-billable time and workspace results.

All of it is workspace-scoped and permission-aware, for solo work or a small team.

## Self-hosted & open source

**Heading:** Run it yourself. Own your data.

**Body:** Longtail Forge Core is open source under AGPL-3.0-only. Run it on your own server and keep your workspace data on infrastructure you control. Migrations run automatically on start; your data lives in a directory you own.

Self-hosting means you're responsible for your deployment — the server, TLS, backups, updates, and access. Longtail Forge gives you the app and the documentation; a safe deployment still depends on following the setup.

An official hosted version and managed private instances are planned for later, for people who'd rather not run a server. `[No date or price — do not add one.]`

## Trust & data control

**Heading:** Transparent by design.

- **Open core.** The app you run is the open-source AGPL core — you can read exactly what it does.
- **Your data, your server.** Self-hosted installs keep workspace data under your control.
- **Permission-aware and workspace-scoped.** Records, search, and notifications respect who can see what.
- **Audit logging.** Security-relevant actions are recorded for admins.
- **Secure notes, encrypted at rest.** Sensitive notes are encrypted at rest with a server-managed key. This is not "zero-knowledge" — a configured server can decrypt them — so treat it as strong at-rest protection, not a vault for critical secrets.

**Honest status:** Longtail Forge is in active development. Internet-exposure hardening and tested backup/restore are on the near-term roadmap; until that work ships, treat internet use as a private/technical preview, not a production deployment. We don't claim certifications, audits, or guarantees we haven't earned.
