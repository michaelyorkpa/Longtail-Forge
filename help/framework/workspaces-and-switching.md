A workspace is the active scope for most Longtail Forge data. Clients, projects, tasks, time entries, tags, settings, module availability, and search results are read through the current workspace.

Use the workspace switcher in the app shell when your account belongs to more than one workspace. Switching workspaces changes the available records and may change which modules appear.

In User Settings, **User App Preferences** lets you choose where Longtail Forge opens after initial login and after changing workspaces. Each preference can open Dashboard, Workbench, Actions: Tasks, Actions: Notes, or Actions: Lists. If the selected page is not enabled or available to you in the destination workspace, Longtail Forge opens Dashboard instead.

Workspace settings are managed from Settings when your role allows it. The workspace type is chosen when the workspace is created and cannot be changed later; it and enabled modules can affect which tools are visible. A Workspace Administrator or Super Admin can rename the workspace. Use the **Users** action beside the Workspace Settings heading to review workspace users.

A Workspace Administrator or Super Admin can also use **Create Workspace Backup** before a future deletion or recovery decision. The package is limited to the active workspace and its attachment artifacts. Workspace Settings shows the package checksum and recovery summary, but does not offer a browser download. Secure Notes stay encrypted and their master key is never included, so packages containing Secure Notes require the separately protected key backup during operator validation and recovery.

**Delete Workspace** is a separate Workspace Settings action for Workspace Administrators and Super Admins. It requires the exact workspace name plus either a backup created within the previous 24 hours or the displayed `DELETE WITHOUT CURRENT BACKUP` acknowledgement. The request starts a visible 30-day grace period; it does not immediately delete data or disable sessions, memberships, modules, Files, Search, jobs, or notifications. An administrator can cancel before the displayed deadline. Reaching the deadline does not itself delete data; a protected-host operator must deliberately queue final cleanup. Once that process begins, the workspace is fenced, its sessions end, and its records and stored artifacts are irreversibly removed while other workspaces remain outside the deletion scope.

In User Settings, **Leave Workspace** removes only your membership in the selected workspace. It does not delete the workspace or its data, which remain available to other members. A Workspace Administrator or Super Admin must restore your access if you need to return. Normally you must switch before leaving the current workspace and keep at least one active workspace. If you are a Workspace Administrator, workspace owner, or installation Super Admin leaving your final active workspace, ownership must first transfer to another Workspace Administrator; your ordinary sessions then end. Your next sign-in opens only **Account data recovery**, where you can download your account profile and preferences or log out. It cannot recover workspace records, Files, or backups.
