Notifications recover attention after relevant work changes. They do not become Tasks, change record status, or grant access to the linked record.

Notifications appear from the bell icon in the navigation row. The panel shows unread activity and links to the full Notifications page.

Notifications are scoped to the current user and workspace. Read, dismiss, and preference actions affect your notification view without changing other users' notifications.

Some record screens provide a record-level notification bell. In the task dialog, clicking the heading bell follows or unfollows notifications for that task directly. A red task bell means notifications are enabled for that task.

Task due reminders appear as high-priority notifications for assigned users, or for the task creator when the task has no assignee. Following a task also sends its due reminder notifications to you. Tasks owns reminder timing and eligibility; Notifications owns permission-shaped delivery and each recipient’s row.

Notification preferences are managed through shared settings surfaces. Module events can create notifications only through their registered contribution. Delivery rechecks current workspace, module, recipient, and preference context. Links are hidden when you cannot access the target record, and dismissing or marking a notification read does not change that record.

Use Notifications when you need to review what called for attention. Use Workbench when you are ready to choose and act on startable work. See the Tasks-owned **Reminders, Calendar, and Subscriptions** article when Tasks is active.
